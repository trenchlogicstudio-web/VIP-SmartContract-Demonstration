VIP SmartContract — Compliance-as-Revenue Layer

A guest chooses a gaming budget and reviews the amounts before accepting. At the agreed line, Hard-Stop blocks checked play. The operator’s eligibility decision determines whether any of the remaining budget becomes resort or future-play credit. The receipt shows how much stays with the guest.
