11 · Competitive Battlecard — including the patent landscape, named first

Named competitors, exact lines

Wallets that already enforce, platforms that already limit, CRMs that already campaign, detection that already flags, crypto operators that already verify — and the patents. Every boundary stated by us before the buyer's diligence states it back.

SHIPS IN THE PACKAGE. Name, ownership and status of IGT, Sightline, Optimove, Mindway and Kambi re-verified 23 September 2026; OpenBet, Fast Track and Future Anthem carry no check of that date and stand for their class only. Cashless wallets (IGT / Sightline class)

Limits + non-gaming spend exist here · enforcement without economics
What they do
Unified funding across gaming and non-gaming with spending-limit management — limit enforcement and resort-wide spend are real, shipped capabilities in this class.
Where the line sits
Their limits are hard stops inside a payments product; none pays the player for respecting the limit, and none converts preserved budget into scheduled non-gaming revenue. Enforcement exists; the economics do not — that sentence is the asset's whole position, and it survives an informed buyer because it concedes everything true first.
Your line
"Wallets can already stop a player. Nothing on the floor pays them for stopping and books the evening with what they kept. We sell the second sentence — and we work beside any wallet you run."

Platform limit stacks (Kambi / OpenBet class)

Deposit & loss limits at platform level
What they do
Mandated limit tooling in regulated platform stacks — table stakes, jurisdiction-driven.
Where the line sits
Limits as compliance cost, experienced as punishment. The middleware here turns the identical threshold into a hospitality event with a rollover attached.
Your line
"You already have the threshold. We change what crossing it means — from 'you're done' to 'here's what you earned by stopping.'"

Gaming CRM journeys (Optimove / Fast Track class)

Post-hoc campaigns on registered behaviour
What they do
Lifecycle campaigns reacting to behaviour after it lands in the warehouse — excellent retention machinery, hours-to-days latency. The class is consolidating: Optimove announced the acquisition of Smartico on 6 April 2026, with both platforms continuing to operate independently (verified 23 September 2026).
Where the line sits
This engine acts in the transaction path at the moment of threshold, not the morning after. For a CRM-vendor buyer (02b) that real-time policy layer is the missing product line, not the rival.
Your line
"Your journeys start when the data lands. This acts while the player is still at the table — and then hands your journeys a cleaner, retained customer."

Risk-detection AI (Mindway / Future Anthem class)

Detection without transaction-path economics
What they do
Real-time behavioural risk scoring and early-warning flags — strong science, advisory output.
Where the line sits
Detection tells the operator something is wrong; it does not stop, pay, or redirect. The two layers compose: their flag can raise this engine's policy.
Your line
"Detection is the smoke alarm. This is the sprinkler that also comps the dinner. Run both."

Provably-fair crypto operators

Verifiable trust exists here — different market, different claim
What they do
Crypto-native operators have competed on verifiable trust for years — provably fair outcomes, on-chain transparency. The concept of trust-by-verification is not new and this package does not claim to have invented it.
Where the line sits
Their verification covers game fairness for a crypto-native audience. This asset's verification covers policy decisions for regulated land-based and omni operators whose players never see a chain — middleware anchoring proofs, never funds.
Your line
"Provably fair proved players value verification. We bring the verification to the regulated floor without bringing the crypto — proofs anchored, funds untouched."

Patent landscape — stated, not discovered

On-chain RG limit enforcement is patent-encumbered
What exists
Granted US patents cover blockchain-based management and enforcement of responsible-gaming limits across properties. Any buyer's counsel will find them in minutes — which is why this card names the landscape first.
Where this asset stands
Scoped to the incentive-economics layer; enforcement and funds deliberately off-chain; only proofs hash-anchored. The rejection of on-chain enforcement is a documented design decision (10), not an omission.
Your line
"The on-chain enforcement space is patented — so we don't live there, on purpose. Our claims sit in the economics layer, and we recommend your counsel run FTO before any exclusivity. You're hearing that from us, not finding it after the LOI."
Objection quick-fire · verbatim answers

When the buyer says —

ObjectionAnswer
"Blockchain in regulated gaming is a non-starter."Agreed — which is why funds and rules never touch a chain here. This is middleware; the chain only timestamps proofs of decisions already made. Your regulator reads an audit trail, not a wallet.
"Wallets already enforce limits."They do, and the card above says so by name. What no wallet does is pay the player for stopping and convert the preserved budget into spa, dinner, and retail revenue. Enforcement is commodity; the economics are the asset.
"What about the patents?"Named in this package before you asked. On-chain enforcement is encumbered; this asset is scoped to the incentive layer and keeps enforcement off-chain by documented design. FTO before exclusivity is our recommendation, in writing, in 01 and here.
"VIPs will walk when stopped."In the baseline they do — in the model, 39% of the players who hit a stop are lost within the quarter. The rollover and the booked evening cut that to 16% of them. The stop that pays is the retention mechanism, not the threat to it.
"Isn't redirecting gaming budget revenue suicide?"The baseline alternative isn't the player losing the rest — it's the player leaving with it. 70% of preserved budget captured in the building beats 0% in a taxi, and the lifetime stays on your book.
"Where's the pilot?"None, by declaration — figures labelled synthetic, parameters printed, policy engine running in your browser (05b). Interrogate it.
