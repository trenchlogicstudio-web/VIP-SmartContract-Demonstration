The four demos

What each one proves

05a · Host Console

The host’s screen, six clickable steps: flag, consent, policy, arm, trigger, redirect — one VIP walked from risk to retained.

05b · Policy Engine

A live session against a real policy: set the stop-loss, deal the shoe, watch the policy fire and the preserved budget settle into resort credit, rollover and the guest’s own residual — then flip to the hard-stop-only baseline and watch retention pay the difference.

05c · Ledger Model

The quarter economics, recomputable. The reference preset reproduces the canon's quarter figures exactly: 84% vs 61% retention of limited VIPs, €214K redirected at 70% capture of preserved budget.

05d · Narrative Proof

One synthetic resort quarter, event by event. Click any point and the KPI board morphs to that week’s state.
