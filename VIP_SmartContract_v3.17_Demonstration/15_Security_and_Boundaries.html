15 · Security and production boundaries

What is built, and what is deliberately yours

An evaluation artifact that pretends to be production software wastes a reviewer's afternoon and loses their trust in the parts that are real. This page draws the line explicitly.

The rule that governs this page. Everything under IMPLEMENTED is behaviour you can run and verify today, and the test suite asserts it. Everything under YOURS is absent ON PURPOSE — not missing, not planned, not a roadmap item. A policy layer licensed as-is cannot carry your identity provider, your network boundary or your operational runbook, and a package that claimed to would be making a promise nobody is left to keep.

Implemented and verified

What the engine actually does

AreaWhat holds, and how you can check it
Settlement integrity A budget is taken to the nearest cent on arrival and consumption is read in whole cents from its exact total; money is divided in integer cents by largest-remainder allocation, a tie going to the earlier position, and every configured fraction is read as an integer number of millionths. Two invariants hold for every amount: the three venue credits sum exactly to the redirect leg, and redirect plus rollover plus residual sums exactly to the preserved budget. The suite asserts this EXHAUSTIVELY across every amount from €0.01 to €2,000.00, and again with the reward cap engaged, rather than on a sample — and it recomputes the rule itself on every one of those amounts, because a split can close and still put a cent into the wrong venue.
Published limits The stop line the pre-commit call publishes is the first whole cent at or above the threshold share of the budget, and the engine fires on exactly that cent — not a cent later. The nudge and escalation lines are the same computation. The suite checks every line on every budget from €0.01 to €2,000.00.
Input validation Every threshold, split, port and lifetime is validated at process start and a value out of range STOPS the process rather than degrading it, with one sentence naming the value; every value the validation accepts starts, seeds and serves. A budget runs from €0.01 to €10,000,000, and a consumption delta must be finite, non-negative and at most €10,000,000. Determination timestamps must parse. Unknown eligibility values, suppression reasons and modes are refused with named identifiers, and so is malformed input: a body that is not a JSON object, a request target that cannot be read, or an identifier of the wrong type is answered with its identifier, never with a server error.
Tamper evidence Every decision is a hash-chained, append-only event. A settlement that will not issue — at the stop, when a suppression lands on it before it was issued, or at the issue call when the determination lapsed in between — is written as its own link, once, and so is a removal from the programme; a lapse and a deliberate suppression are recorded as different facts. A verification route recomputes the chain.
Ordering safety The guards hold in orders nobody designed for, and the suite proves it: suppression after a stop, issue before a stop, a replayed issue, suppression before enrolment, and a cap combined with an expired determination. A negative delta cannot walk a session back below a stop it has already reached, and once a guest is suppressed nothing issued earlier can be collected: a credit is not redeemed and a rollover is not applied.
Schema upgrade The migration runs at server start, so a database written by an earlier build gains the columns this one reads. Asserted against a deliberately older schema.
Data minimisation One column in the whole system holds a name: the display name. Everything else stored about a guest — tier, budgets and consumption, the eligibility determination your platform supplies, and a suppression with its reason — is keyed to the opaque identifier your platform assigns, and the chain carries that identifier, never the name. Erasure removes the name from the database file and its write-ahead log, not only from the API, and the suite reads both files as bytes after an erasure to prove it. Whether the rows keyed to the identifier must go as well is a legal question for your jurisdiction.
Console output Every string the evaluation console prints passes through one escape function, and its buttons carry their values in data attributes rather than in inline script, so an identifier armed as markup is shown as text and never runs in the host's browser.
Deliberately absent

What is yours, and what breaks if it is skipped

BoundaryWhy it is not hereWhat happens if nobody does it
Authentication The evaluation instance ships a single static admin token so a reviewer can open the console in one step. It is auth for an evaluation, not for a property. Anyone who can reach the port can read the host and admin views.
Authorisation The engine has no concept of a staff role. Who may open a session, who may suppress a guest and who may see a settlement are your policy decisions, and they differ per property. Every caller can perform every operation, including removing a guest from the programme.
A gaming-day lock The engine locks the session it stops: every further tick is refused and nothing lifts the stop. The budget is the day's by agreement, but the engine keeps no calendar, so a new pre-commit for the same guest opens a new session. Whether a stopped guest may commit again the same day is your rule, and it belongs where you take the pre-commit. A guest stopped tonight can be armed again tonight with a fresh budget.
Transport and network boundary The service speaks plain HTTP on a local port. TLS termination, network placement and rate limiting belong to your infrastructure. Traffic is readable in transit and the service is reachable by anything that can route to it.
Idempotency of inbound events THIS IS THE ONE A REVIEWER WILL RAISE FIRST, so it is stated plainly rather than buried. The engine has no event identifier and no replay protection. A gaming event delivered twice by your bus is processed twice. Deduplication is stateful infrastructure with a retention policy and a rule for what a repeated identifier carrying a DIFFERENT body means — decisions that belong to the integrator, not to a policy layer licensed as-is. Double-counted consumption, and a stop that fires earlier than the guest's real spend justifies. Make the boundary idempotent before the engine sees the event.
Persistence and durability A single local database file, suitable for one evaluation instance. No replication, no backup, no retention schedule. The audit chain is exactly as durable as one file on one disk.
Scale and concurrency Written to be read and verified, not to be load-bearing. Single process, no horizontal story. It will not carry a floor. This is a reference implementation of a mechanism, and the canon says so.
The risk assessment itself This layer holds no harm model and forms no view of any guest. It consumes your determination and records that it honoured it. A gate is only as good as the judgement it is given.
Certification and change control Where a regime requires independent laboratory certification of systems touching player limits, a deployment enters that perimeter. A go-live that has not been through it.
