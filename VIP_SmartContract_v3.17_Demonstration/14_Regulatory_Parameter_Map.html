14 · Regulatory parameter map

Which lever answers which question

Every restriction this architecture has to survive is a setting, not a rewrite. This page names the restriction, the parameter that answers it, and what changes when you move it.

This is not legal advice. Trench Logic Studio is not a law firm and gives none. This map is written for your compliance function to take to your counsel and your regulator — it tells them where the levers are, not whether your configuration is lawful in your jurisdiction. That determination is yours.

The objection to answer first

The incentive is earned by stopping, not by playing

The sharpest question this design will be asked is that the incentive arrives at the moment the budget is exhausted — which is the moment a regulator watches most closely. It deserves a direct answer rather than a disclaimer.

The answer is structural. An incentive that is released only when play ends cannot reward chasing losses, cannot reward extended play, and cannot reward accelerating frequency, because every one of those behaviours moves the guest away from it rather than toward it. A scheme whose status is earned by spending more is the thing the guidance in several jurisdictions is written against. This is its inverse.

One residual exposure is real and the package names it rather than hiding it: if the reward scales without limit with the pre-committed budget, then a larger declared budget earns a larger reward, and that is an incentive to declare a larger budget. VIP_REWARD_CAP_EUR exists for exactly that objection.

What configuration does not fix

Four things the buyer owns

Certification and change control

In jurisdictions that require independent laboratory certification of gaming systems, a deployment that touches player limits falls inside that perimeter. Expect a certification or change-management review before go-live, and budget time for it per jurisdiction rather than once.

The risk assessment itself

This engine holds no harm model and forms no view of any guest. It consumes your determination and records that it honoured it. Whatever produces that determination — your own analytics, a detection vendor, a host's judgement, a register feed — remains yours, and detection systems are a complement to this layer rather than a competitor to it.

Where the reference configuration sits

Every figure in the canon was computed with the gate open and the cap unset. A stricter configuration lowers realised retention and realised redirect in that deployment. It does not make any published figure wrong: the figures state what the mechanism produces at a stated configuration, and the configuration is printed beside them.

The determination cannot be undone here

Suppression is not reversible by this engine, by design. Restoring a guest to a programme after an exclusion or a withdrawal is a decision with its own governance and its own sign-off, and a policy layer is the wrong place to make it silently.

Stated rather than left to be found

Known limits of this release

A package that names its own limits reads as prepared. One that leaves them for the buyer's reviewer to find reads as unexamined. These are properties, not warnings about any particular market.

Advisory mode notifies; it does not enforce

At the thresholds, advisory mode raises a nudge and an escalation and leaves the decision with the guest and the host. That is what the mode is for. But a notification without enforcement has been held insufficient by at least one supervisory authority, and a licensee choosing advisory mode should satisfy itself that notification meets its own obligations. Hard-stop mode enforces. Both run the same engine and the same thresholds.

The engine forms no view of risk

This layer performs no risk assessment and holds no harm model. It consumes your determination and records that it honoured it. Whatever produces that determination remains yours — and so does its quality. A gate is only as good as the judgement it is given.

Automated decisions are being governed more tightly

Expectations for automated decision-making are tightening across regulated industries: decision provenance, a named human owner, constrained behaviour, retained logs. This engine is deterministic, fixed by its canon, and writes an append-only chain that includes its refusals — which is the shape those expectations describe. Nothing is claimed here about whether any specific regime classifies this system, and no such claim should be made on this package's behalf.

A determination has an age

Until v3.8 the operator's eligibility determination was honoured indefinitely. It now carries the timestamp of when it was made, and a configurable lifetime after which the gate closes until a fresh one arrives. Shipped unset, so nothing changes unless you set it.
