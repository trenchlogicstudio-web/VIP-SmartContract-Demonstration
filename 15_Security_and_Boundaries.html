15 · Security and production boundaries

What is built, and what is deliberately yours

An evaluation artifact that pretends to be production software wastes a reviewer's afternoon and loses their trust in the parts that are real. This page draws the line explicitly.

The rule that governs this page. Everything under IMPLEMENTED is behaviour you can run and verify today, and the test suite asserts it. Everything under YOURS is absent ON PURPOSE — not missing, not planned, not a roadmap item. A policy layer released as is cannot carry your identity provider, your network boundary or your operational runbook, and a package that claimed to would be making a promise nobody is left to keep.

Implemented and verified

What the engine actually does

AreaWhat holds, and how you can check it
Settlement integrity A budget is taken to the nearest cent on arrival and consumption is read in whole cents from its exact total; money is divided in integer cents by largest-remainder allocation, a tie going to the earlier position, and every configured fraction is read as an integer number of millionths. Two invariants hold for every amount: the three venue credits sum exactly to the redirect leg, and redirect plus rollover plus residual sums exactly to the preserved budget. The suite asserts this EXHAUSTIVELY across every amount from €0.01 to €2,000.00, and again with the reward cap engaged, rather than on a sample — and it recomputes the rule itself on every one of those amounts, because a split can close and still put a cent into the wrong venue.
Published limits The stop line the pre-commit call publishes is the first whole cent at or above the threshold share of the budget, and the engine fires when consumption reaches that cent. Whether consumption can pass it is decided in the wager path, in the next row. The nudge and escalation lines are the same computation. The suite checks every line on every budget from €0.01 to €2,000.00.
The stop line, where the stake is checked The pre-stake check reserves every stake the platform asks about against what is left to the line, as if it were lost, and in Hard-Stop refuses one that does not fit before it is placed; the refusal is its own link in the chain and stays there although the request that caused it fails. Each check and its reservation are one step, so twelve stakes of 300 sent at once against a line of 1,000 get exactly three through. The suite runs 2,000 seeded sessions with every stake checked, and consumption never once passes the line. It also keeps the case the check exists for: 1,500 reported without a check at 1,400 consumed against a line of 1,500 counts in full, and the stop records the 1,400 past the line as overshoot_eur. A report delivered twice against one check counts once.
The commitment period While a session is live and younger than VIP_COMMITMENT_HOURS (24 by default), a new pre-commit for the same guest is refused with SESSION_LIVE — a larger, an equal and a smaller budget alike — and the session, its budget and its line are untouched. From v3.22 a stopped session holds the guest for the same period, counted from when it was armed, and a new pre-commit is refused with COMMITMENT_ACTIVE. Until v3.19 one such call replaced the session and moved the line in the middle of play, and until v3.22 a stopped guest could arm a second budget at once. The suite checks both refusals in both services, the release after the period, a clock set backwards, and that 0 restores the old behaviour.
The terms a guest agreed to GET /v3/terms publishes the terms — the line, the split, the ceiling, the determination lifetime and the commitment period — with a version that is a fingerprint of exactly those values. Every pre-commit records that version beside the amount, in the session and as a link of the chain, and a pre-commit that names another version is refused with TERMS_CHANGED before anything is armed. Both services compute the same version for the same terms, and the suite checks it.
Input validation Every threshold, split, port and lifetime is validated at process start and a value out of range STOPS the process rather than degrading it, with one sentence naming the value; every value the validation accepts starts, seeds and serves. A budget runs from €0.01 to €10,000,000, and a consumption delta must be finite, non-negative and at most €10,000,000. Determinations must be valid, past or current timestamps with a time zone. Unknown eligibility values, suppression reasons and modes are refused with named identifiers, and so is malformed input: a body that is not a JSON object, a request target that cannot be read, or an identifier of the wrong type is answered with its identifier, never with a server error.
Tamper evidence Every decision is a hash-chained, append-only event. A settlement that will not issue — at the stop, when a suppression lands on it before it was issued, or at the issue call when the determination lapsed in between — is written as its own link, once, and so is a removal from the programme; a lapse and a deliberate suppression are recorded as different facts. A verification route recomputes the chain.
Ordering safety The guards hold in orders nobody designed for, and the suite proves it: suppression after a stop, issue before a stop, a replayed issue, suppression before enrolment, and a cap combined with an expired determination. A negative delta cannot walk a session back below a stop it has already reached, and once a guest is suppressed nothing issued earlier can be collected: a credit is not redeemed and a rollover is not applied.
Schema upgrade The migration runs at server start, so a database written by an earlier build gains the columns this one reads. Asserted against a deliberately older schema.
Data minimisation One column in the whole system holds a name: the display name. Everything else stored about a guest — tier, budgets and consumption, the eligibility determination your platform supplies, and a suppression with its reason — is keyed to the opaque identifier your platform assigns, and the chain carries that identifier, never the name. Erasure removes the name from the database file and its write-ahead log, not only from the API, and the suite reads both files as bytes after an erasure to prove it. Whether the rows keyed to the identifier must go as well is a legal question for your jurisdiction.
Console output Every string the evaluation console prints passes through one escape function, and its buttons carry their values in data attributes rather than in inline script, so an identifier armed as markup is shown as text and never runs in the host's browser.
Deliberately absent

What is yours, and what breaks if it is skipped

BoundaryWhy it is not hereWhat happens if nobody does it
The wager path The engine is not in your wager path, so it holds the line only where your platform asks before it accepts each stake (POST /v3/session/authorize) and places only what is authorized. What counts as consumption — the stake, or the net loss — is your definition, stated once; the line holds while no wager consumes more than the stake checked for it. This build checks and reserves in one step in one process; several instances serving one guest's session need the check and the reservation in one transaction on a shared store. Consumption reaches the engine after the fact: the session locks on the tick that crosses the line, that tick counts in full, and the stop lands past the line by the size of that report — recorded as overshoot_eur, never hidden.
Authentication The evaluation instance ships a single static admin token so a reviewer can open the console in one step. It is auth for an evaluation, not for a property. Host and guest operations are available to local callers. The admin page and erasure require the evaluation token.
Authorisation The engine has no concept of a staff role. Who may open a session, who may suppress a guest and who may see a settlement are your policy decisions, and they differ per property. Every caller can perform every operation, including removing a guest from the programme.
A gaming-day lock The engine locks the session it stops: every further tick is refused and nothing lifts the stop. From v3.22 it also holds the guest for the rest of the commitment period, counted from when the session was armed, so a second budget is refused. It keeps no calendar: the period is counted in hours from arming, not by your gaming day, and it holds only the pre-commits that reach this engine. A gaming day that ends at a fixed hour, and one limit across tables, terminals and other channels, belong where you take the pre-commit. The guest is held for 24 hours from arming rather than to the end of your gaming day, and only on the channels that send their pre-commits here.
Transport and network boundary The service speaks plain HTTP on a local port. TLS termination, network placement and rate limiting belong to your infrastructure. Direct startup binds to loopback by default. The Compose ports publish on 127.0.0.1. An explicitly exposed listener requires an authenticated gateway and TLS.
Idempotency of inbound events Supply a stable event_id and the pre-commit session_id on a tick. A replay of the same delta returns the stored response, including the original 423. A different delta with the same identifier returns 409 EVENT_CONFLICT. SQLite keeps these receipts across restarts. The reference keeps them in memory. Ticks without an event_id count each delivery. A pre-commit is not a replay-safe call: repeated inside the commitment period it is refused, with SESSION_LIVE while the session is live and COMMITMENT_ACTIVE once it has stopped, and after the period it opens a new session. Downstream delivery is best effort.
Persistence and durability A single local database file, suitable for one evaluation instance. No replication, no backup, no retention schedule. The audit chain is exactly as durable as one file on one disk.
Scale and concurrency Written to be read and verified, not to be load-bearing. Single process, no horizontal story — and the pre-stake check relies on that, as the wager path above states. It will not carry a floor. This is a reference implementation of a mechanism, and the canon says so.
The risk assessment itself This layer holds no harm model and forms no view of any guest. It consumes your determination and records that it honoured it. A gate is only as good as the judgement it is given.
Certification and change control Where a regime requires independent laboratory certification of systems touching player limits, a deployment enters that perimeter. A go-live that has not been through it.
